# Leave and People operations business rules

## Confirmed

- Safer People System covers MAM and Equipsy.
- Managers and team leads need a shared view of who is away so coverage does not depend on asking around in person.
- The initial release remains a management review before all-staff rollout.
- Nixon owns delivery priority; team-supplied priority is input, not a command.
- Employee and HR information is confidential.
- Every authenticated employee may use the organisation tree. It shows the name, position, company and reporting relationship required to understand the structure, and may reveal only a selected person’s work email.
- Employee contact, employment and equipment-allocation details are held in a private one-to-one HR record. The organisation tree never exposes phone numbers, personal email addresses, dates of birth, laptop/asset records or other private employee fields.
- Every active directory-linked employee may open the top-right self-profile. It may show only that employee’s own phone number, personal email, date of birth and read-only work email; they may update only their own phone number, personal email and date of birth. An actual HR administrator using the sidebar Change view control may instead load one selected real employee through an independently HR-authorised profile path and correct those same three private contact fields. The selected employee never gains HR access, and a non-HR browser cannot use that path.
- The database-controlled **System Admin** role is an all-module role. Approved System Admin accounts are configured only in the verified production role registry. They may view and administer the full employee directory, accounts, organisation, check-ins, leave and calendar; a browser-selected profile or client state never grants these permissions.
- Employee profiles are archived before permanent deletion. Archiving requires an employment end date and a controlled reason, removes the person from active directory views while retaining history, and disables the linked portal account as part of the same confirmed offboarding action. Archived records are visible only to authorised HR and global reviewers. Legacy inactive roster rows without an archive reason are not archived profiles and do not appear in the Archived filter. Permanent deletion is limited to archived, unlinked, history-free profiles.
- Approved legacy leave may be imported against the stable employee-directory identity. Current employees retain ownership of their imported records; former employees remain archived without a login. A source-row digest prevents duplicate imports, imports never send workflow email, and an unknown legacy employment end date remains blank instead of being invented.
- The HR Directory is the unified employee editor. Its committed name, title, company, reporting and start-date changes update the canonical directory and are distributed to authorised sessions through Realtime.
- The live organisation baseline keeps every active Team Leader directly reporting to the MAM Director. Under the Minor Mods Team Leader, five structural groups—Scheduling team, Estimating team, Trades team, Admin team and CSO team—keep the approved Minor Mods reporting view clear. CSO stays empty until HR assigns a confirmed employee; it must never be filled by title inference.
- Employee self-service contact changes update the same private employee-detail record but do not change the public organisation tree, work identity, work email, reporting relationship or account settings.
- The HR CSV preserves the agreed 13 employee columns and field order, with Company immediately after Team, then appends Laptop supplied, Laptop ID, Mouse ID, Keyboard ID and Asset Tags. It excludes portal logins, Auth aliases, existing passwords and any newly issued password.
- MAM uses the orange accent and Equipsy uses blue; company remains visible as text.
- Real roster names and positions are imported privately into the verified production project. They must not be committed to Git, copied into fixtures or used in screenshots.
- A supplied HR workbook must be staged and reconciled with the live directory before import. It does not authorise automatic identity replacement, deactivation or portal-account relinking.
- Every ordinary portal account must link to one active person in the real directory. There are no fake or synthetic selectable profiles or demo roles. **Change profile** lists active real directory people only for a controlled System Admin account.
- The server verifies the signed-in account and its active status before opening the application. The signed-in session and database-controlled role are the only source of access; client-side state never grants permission or impersonates another account. Change profile changes the employee-workspace presentation and manager-safe Check-in Calendar/task scope, while the real System Admin retains independently re-authorised functions for testing (including leave submission, evidence, cancellation, decisions and check-in completion). Every mutation records the real System Admin actor; selecting a profile never grants those permissions to that employee.
- An authorised HR user may view account login names, status and last sign-in time; create a login; change a login name; set a new password; and disable or enable an account.
- No HR user can view or recover an existing password. Supabase Auth stores password hashes, not reversible passwords. A newly generated or supplied password may be shown once after creation/reset and is not retained in application tables or logs.
- The deployed durable leave flow requires every submitted request to start Pending. It has no automatic approval and no self-approval. Approval authority is a separate, private database capability: an active linked profile with the database `manager` role is eligible, and the organisation tree determines which manager receives each request. Job titles and team labels alone never grant approval.
- Every active linked employee may submit their own leave request. Manager-owned requests route to the explicitly configured Penny, Steven and HR reviewer group; each receives the email and any one may approve through their own account. Other employee requests walk upward through the reporting tree and stops at the nearest active manager profile. An employee who is not a manager does not intercept requests from people below them. Every System Admin may review every request as part of their all-module role.
- At submission, the database snapshots the configured reviewer group for manager-owned requests, or the nearest manager and global reviewers for other employees. Still-pending work is re-routed when manager permission changes; decided history is not rewritten. No reviewer, including HR and global reviewers, may decide their own leave request.
- An active linked employee may cancel any of their own leave requests, regardless of leave type, date or current status, unless it is already cancelled. System Admin retains the same capability while using Change Profile. Cancellation is an audited state transition: the request and any previous decision remain in history, and an already cancelled request cannot be cancelled again.
- Employment type is recorded as ABN, Casual, Full Time or Part Time by a System Admin. Full Time and Part Time employees may request Annual, Sick, Carer's or Unpaid leave. ABN and Casual employees have no paid-leave entitlement and may request Unpaid leave only. The database snapshots the employment type and paid/unpaid treatment when each request is submitted; this classification is eligibility, not a leave-balance calculation.
- A System Admin may record an employee's usual working weekdays. Every active linked employee can see their own schedule and submit one pending change request with a required reason; Change profile offers the same workflow while preserving the real System Admin's full review authority and audit identity. The nearest active Manager in the current reporting line reviews the request, and both approval and decline require a decision reason. Review scope is recalculated at decision time, the account that submitted a request cannot approve or decline it, approval cannot overwrite any newer direct administrator edit, and archiving an employee automatically closes their pending request. The employee retains a scoped change history. This workflow does not send email. An empty weekday schedule means it has not been confirmed, and the field does not yet alter leave duration, check-in milestones, payroll or calendar calculations.
- Every active employee receives an automatic check-in schedule from their start date. Users do not enable reminders or queue catch-up work manually.
- If a check-in milestone due date falls on Saturday or Sunday, it moves forward to the next Monday-to-Friday working day. Reminder lead time is measured from that scheduled workday; if the resulting advance notification date falls on a weekend, it moves back to the previous working day. Due check-in tasks appear in Calendar.
- Every System Admin may view and complete check-ins for all employees. Configured managers receive Check-in access only for their current descendants in the authoritative organisation tree. This scope is based on the controlled active portal-account-to-employee link, not Auth metadata or a job title. An incorrect reporting link can therefore grant the wrong scoped task list and must be treated as an HR data-integrity issue. Manager access never exposes HR private profile fields.
- Check-in reminders due before 1 July 2026 are backfilled as complete and must not generate catch-up work or email.
- Creating an employee in the HR Directory does not create a portal login, invitation or email. HR creates a login separately through the authorised Accounts workflow; automated employee invitations are not available.

## Decisions required before live workflow use

1. Who may submit leave on behalf of another employee?
2. What delegate or fallback procedure applies when a named reviewer is unavailable? Any delegation must be an explicit private approval grant; the application must not infer it from the organisation tree.
3. What overlap information may employees see: names and dates, team totals only, or nothing?
4. Which additional leave categories, if any, are supported and which reasons are private to HR?
5. Are weekends, public holidays, part-time patterns and break deductions calculated automatically?
6. Which systems receive an approved result: Outlook, payroll, AroFlo, Teams or none initially?
7. What is the retention period for rejected/cancelled requests, evidence and event history?
8. What counts as manager-ready versus all-staff-ready?
9. Who may start, view and complete onboarding/offboarding work, including delegated owners and HR-only exit information?
10. Which systems, assets and subscriptions are required for each role, who approves them, and what retention/audit period applies? **Interim implementation pending confirmation/deployment:** only a System Admin receives the full asset and subscription registers (including costs and membership); employees retain their own minimised subscription/device projections, and managers receive no company-wide register until a scoped policy is approved.
11. Should a cross-company reporting-line edit require an additional confirmation?
12. What credential handover and forced-password-change process is required when an administrator creates or resets an account?
13. Payroll balances are supplied as dated snapshots. The owner confirmed on 10 September that the supplied MAM and Equipsy workbooks use hours. Reconcile each employee within their company before importing; unmatched names require confirmation. The existing temporary 80-hour fallback remains only until an authoritative balance is supplied and never triggers an accrual reminder or payroll deduction.

## Payroll balance visibility and reminders — updated 15 September 2026

- Staff see their own annual leave balance. Active team leaders with database Manager permission see annual and sick balances for their scoped employees, including employees whose pending requests they are authorised to review. Staff cannot retrieve sick balances. System Admin retains its existing administration capability; an employee preview does not reveal sick balances.
- The approval page shows the selected employee's annual and authorised sick balance with each snapshot date. System Admin or an explicit active payroll-import delegate may update balances through the staged import workflow; manual balance editing remains System Admin-only. Updates retain an audit history and four decimal places of hours.
- On the first calendar day of each month, the Leave Calendar shows the authorised importer that the MAM and Equipsy payroll snapshots are due. The Calendar reminder becomes complete only after both companies have a current-month imported snapshot. Separately, during the 08:00–16:59 Melbourne delivery window on every Monday-to-Friday date, the MAM n8n workflow sends at most one email to the explicitly configured Gerard person record while either current-month snapshot remains missing. It uses the current HR work email only after revalidating his active portal link and payroll-import grant, names only the company snapshots still required, and sends no HR or Accounts copy. Same-day retries reuse one stable delivery key; the next workday receives a new key. Saturdays and Sundays create no email, public holidays are not modelled, and the series stops as soon as both snapshots are current.
- Payroll CSV/Excel files must be staged before commit. The server accepts a small bounded set of `.csv` or non-macro `.xlsx` files, groups annual and sick/personal rows across files, and shows the source date, company, supplied balances and match outcome before applying anything. The source files are not retained; only validated metadata, a digest, staged rows and audit history are stored privately.
- If an export contains multiple snapshot dates for one employee, each source row remains in the audit register and only the newest applicable annual and sick value in that batch can update the current balance. Repeating an unchanged current snapshot is a visible no-op and does not duplicate balance history.
- Matching is exact after case, spacing and punctuation normalisation and is always constrained to the file's company. Only one active same-company person may be linked. Inactive, unmatched, cross-company and ambiguous names remain held for review; the system never guesses a nickname, changes company, creates a person or reactivates a record.
- `Approved – pending leave balance` pauses routine reminders. A later payroll snapshot dated on or after conditional approval, covering the recorded required hours, triggers one manager reminder at the existing working-day schedule. Temporary balances, missing hours and insufficient balances do not trigger it. A failed send retries with the same delivery ID across days and rechecks sufficiency; a successful reminder is not repeated.
- Full-day request duration remains in days. A reviewer records the paid hours required for the reminder; the system does not invent a standard hours-per-day conversion. Timed requests may use their existing recorded hours. Recording reminder hours does not change payroll duration or deduct balances.
- Sufficient balance does not automatically approve leave. The manager still reviews and makes the final decision. The reminder compares the individual request with the supplied balance; it does not reserve hours across competing requests or forecast accrual.
- No live payroll sync is configured by this change. Payroll must supply refreshed balances for future accrual to be detected. The supplied snapshots are dated 10 September 2026; import and release require the project's production approval process.
- The owner authorised the original full release and import on 10 September. Every supplied payroll record is retained in the private import register, including unmatched, inactive, company-mismatched and ambiguous rows. Imported snapshots/history reference the source record and the real importing account instead of attributing the operation to an impersonated portal account. Granting the self-service capability is a separate audited System Admin action and is not hard-coded to a person's name.

## Priority leave workflow — 7 September 2026

- An active Manager may submit and attach evidence for employees whose nearest eligible reporting Manager is that account. Super Admin retains authorised selected-profile capabilities. The employee remains the request owner; the real submitter is audited. Existing reviewer routing and no-self-approval rules remain in force.
- Only Super Admin can manually edit leave balances. The controlled staged-import path may also be used by an explicit active payroll delegate. The policy above expands Manager visibility to scoped employee balances and adds sick balances restricted to leaders. Change profile uses the selected employee's visibility while retaining independently authorised administrator editing. The balance section appears below Requests and balances also appear beside approval decisions. Updates retain an append-only history; temporary values do not calculate payroll.
- `Approved – pending leave balance` means coverage is approved but paid leave is not final. It stays pending in the underlying payroll-facing record until a reviewer confirms the balance and approves. Authorised calendars show it yellow; peers retain availability-only visibility.
- Sick evidence is due on the Monday strictly after the last sick-leave date. The form and detail show the calculated date. After that date, missing evidence prompts review as Unpaid leave; the system does not silently change pay treatment. Leave duration continues to count weekdays only (Monday–Friday; no new holiday calendar is assumed).
- At 08:00 Australia/Melbourne each workday, ordinary pending requests at least three workdays old receive a Manager reminder, once per request per day until handled. The submission date and weekends are excluded from the count. Conditional approvals instead follow the one-time sufficient-balance rule above. These reminders go only to the Manager, without HR or Accounts CC.
- Managers receive a team birthday reminder at 07:00 Australia/Melbourne on the preceding working day (Monday-Friday), with HR copied. Saturday, Sunday and Monday birthdays are reminded on Friday. The daily scheduler runs at 07:00 and 08:00; the database prevents leave reminders before 08:00, retains retry/deduplication, and keeps the actual birthday in the message. Birth years and ages are never sent.
- Submission sends to Manager and employee; approval, conditional approval and decline send to the employee. HR remains copied on every event except leave reminders. Final approval and cancellation of approved/conditionally-approved leave also copy Accounts; cancellation of a merely pending request does not.
- Top-level employees without a reporting Manager route to their explicitly authorised assigned reviewer. No arbitrary manager assignment or guessed address is introduced.
- Reminders have durable claims and completion records. A failed daily run automatically retries after 30 minutes, beyond the 25-minute claim lease; already-sent rows are skipped. Workflow retries use stable event keys. Tests use only synthetic local records and offline mail validation; release verification must not trigger real test mail.

## Durable leave rollout (deployed)

- Migration `20260817120000_persist_leave_workspace` is applied to the verified MAM production project and the matching authenticated application is deployed. Leave requests and decisions persist through controlled, RLS-protected operations and refresh across authorised accounts.
- The interface offers Annual leave, Sick leave, Carer's leave and Unpaid leave to Full Time and Part Time employees. ABN and Casual employees see and may submit Unpaid leave only. An unclassified employee also fails closed to Unpaid leave until a System Admin records the employment type.
- A request may optionally specify a start time for the first date and an end time for the last date. Times are between 6:00 am and 6:00 pm in fixed 15-minute intervals. Leaving both blank means full working days.
- Weekday duration is calculated inside that working window. Break deductions, standard daily hours, payroll treatment and evidence rules are not final policy.
- Every submitted request starts Pending and must be decided by an authorised reviewer. No request is automatically approved or self-approved. The database resolves the nearest active manager profile in the reporting tree, plus every System Admin, and snapshots that reviewer set at submission. Still-pending work is re-routed when manager permission changes; decided history is not rewritten.
- The calendar can browse months and is limited to availability for leave. An ordinary employee peer sees company availability as **Away**, not another person's leave type, reason, notes or evidence. Managers and HR see only the additional detail their authorised review role requires. The approved birthday exception is shown to every signed-in internal employee across MAM and Equipsy as an active employee's name and recurring day/month only; birth year and all other private profile fields remain hidden.
- A submitted or approved leave request may hold up to ten private PDF or supported image attachments of 5 MB each. Files use opaque paths in the private `leave-evidence` bucket, are downloadable only after a fresh request-level authorisation check, and are always forced downloads. A daily `CRON_SECRET`-protected maintenance route removes abandoned objects after a two-hour grace period, expires upload intents and removes minimal upload-attempt rows after 35 days. This release does not provide malware scanning or quarantine; users are warned not to upload unsafe or unrelated files.
- Authorised leave records and decisions persist and refresh across accounts. A submission or cancellation sends one deduplicated HTML notification to the request's server-resolved nearest active manager/admin; approval or decline sends one to every HR-recorded employee address (Work and Personal, when present), with duplicate addresses removed. Every leave notification CCs `hr@mobilityaccess.com.au`; approved and cancelled leave notifications also CC `accounts@mobilityaccess.com.au`. The browser cannot choose a recipient.

## People workflow boundaries

- Recruitment is excluded.
- Check-ins are durable and automatic for active employees. Their due tasks are visible in Calendar only to the authorised global or organisation-subtree reviewers described above.
- Onboarding and offboarding review work must not expose private evidence, HR notes, access details or audit history outside its authorised scope.
- No People workflow action creates a login or provisions or revokes external access.
- An offboarding case is labelled and displayed as offboarding, never onboarding.

## Data minimisation

- The team calendar shows only what is required for coverage: company availability for ordinary employee peers, represented as **Away**. It is not a leave-balance ledger. Birthdays remain name plus recurring month/day only; check-in tasks are a separate entry type visible only within the confirmed reviewer scope.
- Organisation cards show only safe structural information. A selected person may expose their work email; phone number, personal email, date of birth, payroll, leave-reason, medical, work-location and hardware-allocation data never belongs in the employee-visible tree.
- The top-right self-profile is the sole employee self-service private-contact exception: it is scoped to the signed-in employee’s own record. A separate HR-only selected-profile path may show and correct one selected employee’s contact fields after the HR capability is rechecked.
- Detailed leave types, reasons, notes and evidence do not appear in the shared calendar.
- Login pages and account lists do not expose internal Auth email aliases.
- Portal-account passwords are never persisted in application tables, logs, screenshots or source. Existing portal-account passwords are never displayed because Supabase Auth stores only non-reversible hashes.
- Paid subscriptions record seats/licences, whether the MAM credit card is used and a billing anchor date. The server derives the next billing date from the anchor and cycle, including end-of-month handling. Last Reviewed Date comes from the append-only audited review event rather than an editable text field.
- Subscription-service credentials are a separate, explicit vault workflow. Account email, username, password, MFA details, recovery codes, licence key and notes are stored only inside an AES-256-GCM encrypted payload; plaintext is decrypted only after an authorised user explicitly reveals a single service credential, is never included in a catalogue/export/audit row and is cleared from the page after a short timeout. A System Admin controls the named delegate list; a delegate receives no subscription cost, ownership, member or review data merely by receiving credential access.
- HR employee CSV exports are available only to an authorised HR session and are not stored by the application, committed to Git or used as fixtures.
- The business still needs to confirm the long-term retention/deletion period and whether malware scanning or quarantine is required for medical evidence. Until then, access remains restricted to the employee, authorised reviewer and HR, and unnecessary sensitive evidence should not be collected.
- Onboarding/offboarding evidence, exit information, access records and audit history require role-specific access, retention and deletion rules before durable use.
- Production staff data must not be copied into Development or Preview.

### Manager-owned leave routing — confirmed 10 September 2026

- The controlled `manager` account role identifies manager-owned requests. These requests go to Penny, Steven and HR, with all three accounts assigned approval access and all three work addresses included in submission, cancellation and eligible reminder delivery. One authorised decision completes the request; three separate approvals are not required. No self-approval is permitted.
- Membership is a private, release-configured set of verified active portal identities and global approval grants. Addresses come from the linked HR directory. Revoked grants and inactive accounts cannot receive or approve these requests. Ordinary employee routing and System Admin authority remain unchanged.
- Existing submitted manager requests are rerouted; decided history and already-delivered email events are retained. Balance-pending requests retain their reminder pause and one-time sufficiency notification. HR is not duplicated in CC when already a direct recipient.

Approval duration display uses 7.5 hours per full day (confirmed 10 September 2026); hourly requests retain their recorded hours. Annual and sick/personal leave coverage pills compare the relevant visible balance at full precision. Review-held or unavailable balances require checking. This display conversion does not change the 38-hour weekly accrual policy or payroll reconciliation.

### Leave request email balances

Submission emails show requested hours, the current accrued balance, and the balance remaining if that request is approved. Full days use 7.5 hours; an explicit hourly request takes precedence. Values are captured when the request is submitted, so retries use the same figures. Missing, example, or review-held balances show “Check balance”.

Annual leave balances can appear in the employee and reviewer copies. Sick/carer’s leave submission emails are split: only reviewers (including HR) receive the balance; the employee receives a separate confirmation. Each copy has a stable delivery key, and the outbox completes only when every required copy is acknowledged. Existing queued legacy emails retain their original delivery contract. Approval/cancellation emails do not subtract the requested hours again.

### Reviewer balance visibility (11 September 2026)

Every active, currently authorised reviewer of a pending request may see that applicant’s annual and sick leave balances, regardless of the reviewer’s account role. This also applies when System Admin previews the reviewer’s employee workspace. The current request assignment remains authoritative; revoked assignments, inactive accounts and unrelated employees do not gain access. Existing manager visibility and administrator-only balance editing remain unchanged.

The bottom “Leave balances & team requests” management section is available only in the actual Super Admin workspace. It is hidden from employee and reviewer workspaces, including Change profile previews. Reviewers still receive the relevant annual and sick balances beside the specific request they are authorised to review; this does not grant access to the bulk management section.

Employees’ own annual leave balance and the existing authorised manager team-request action appear separately as compact self-service controls. They do not expose the bulk balance list, payroll import register, balance editors or accrual settings.
