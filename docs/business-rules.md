# Leave and People operations business rules

## Canonical role policy

[Portal role permissions](role-permissions.md) defines the approved four ordinary roles (`employee`, `manager`, `account`, `admin`) and independent standalone Super Admin/P0 (`system_admin`). It supersedes historical combined System Admin/HR labels and person-specific leave, Calendar, check-in and payroll capabilities in this document. Account has uniform all-status leave/notes/evidence read access and comparison-only payroll upload, never approval or balance-writing authority. Explicit subscription management and vault delegation remain separate, P0-controlled permissions; reporting, ownership, eligibility and notification routing remain record-scoping data. Only P0 changes roles, selects employee previews and configures security/delegation; Admin retains global daily operational administration.

The queue stores no recipient, message body or review notes; the service resolves the current review details at delivery and n8n fixes the live recipient to People & Culture. Editing an existing HR-path review does not create a duplicate, leaving the pathway cancels an unsent alert, and re-entering it creates a new alert. Historical HR outcomes are not replayed at release.
- Every System Admin may view and complete check-ins for all employees. Configured managers receive Check-in access only for their current descendants in the authoritative organisation tree. This scope is based on the controlled active portal-account-to-employee link, not Auth metadata or a job title. Saturday, Sunday and Monday birthdays are reminded on Friday. - Submission sends to Manager and employee; approval, conditional approval and decline send to the employee. HR remains copied on every leave event except leave reminders. Birthday reminders are also Manager-only with no CC. Final approval and cancellation of approved/conditionally-approved leave also copy Accounts; cancellation of a merely pending request does not.
- Top-level employees without a reporting Manager route to their explicitly authorised assigned reviewer. No arbitrary manager assignment or guessed address is introduced.
- Reminders have durable claims and completion records. A failed daily run automatically retries after 30 minutes, beyond the 25-minute claim lease; already-sent rows are skipped. Workflow retries use stable event keys. Tests use only synthetic local records and offline mail validation; release verification must not trigger real test mail.

## Paid subscription review — confirmed 14 October 2026

Quarterly paid subscription reviews are assigned to all active, explicitly granted Register management delegates, independently of each subscription’s Responsible person. Any one delegate can complete a due review; the shared audited subscription record advances to the next quarter for all delegates. Free and cancelled subscriptions do not generate review tasks. Tasks includes the Subscription reviews filter.

Review reminder emails go to Penny’s configured, verified work address and the current active Register management delegates’ work addresses. One upcoming reminder is sent seven calendar days before review; one due reminder is sent if the review is still incomplete. Each subscription/review-date/phase uses the permanent notification reservation contract. Completed reviews stop reminders for that round. Enabling the reminder start date does not replay earlier overdue reviews. Emails use the existing People Hub theme and link directly to Tasks.

When a confirmed employment archive actually takes effect, each non-cancelled subscription assigned to that person as an access member or Responsible person creates a shared password-change task for the current active Register management delegates. This includes free subscriptions. Future scheduled departures wait until the actual archive; historical departures are not backfilled. Tasks reveals only the service identity and due date. These tasks contain no credentials, departure reason or private exit information and do not send email.
